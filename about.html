About Warmers

A guide written for cold mornings.

Warmers began with a simple question asked every autumn: which jacket should I buy, and will it actually be warm enough?

We answer it by explaining how jackets work. Our guides cover insulation, fit and layering in plain language, and our tools turn your weather and measurements into a clear recommendation.

What we promise

  • Independent. We do not sell jackets or use affiliate links.
  • Explained. Every term we use is defined on the page.
  • Reviewed. Each guide is read by a second editor before publishing, and dated.
